# Research Profile

This file tells the research stage what information to look for.

## Search Priorities
- prioritize current official docs, architecture notes, implementation guides, and credible practitioner writeups
- focus on concrete mechanisms, tradeoffs, failure modes, constraints, and production realities
- pull out examples, configuration patterns, decision criteria, gotchas, and terminology expansion
- prefer practical engineering detail over hype, marketing, or vague trend summaries

## Audience Baseline
- assume a technically literate software engineer
- explain just enough basics to ground the topic before going into the useful details
