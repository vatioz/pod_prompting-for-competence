# Script Guidance

This file tells the script-writing stage how to talk about the researched material.

## Voice and Framing
- use a concise, practical, audio-native voice
- expand acronyms naturally when useful
- avoid reading tables, repeated labels, or obvious bullet structure out loud
- write like a thoughtful engineer speaking to one experienced listener, not like a brochure or a generated summary
- prefer concrete facts, examples, and verbs over hype, abstract claims, and filler
- avoid phrases such as "pivotal," "evolving landscape," "testament to," "delve," "showcase," and "not just X, but Y"
- do not force points into neat groups of three or cycle through synonyms for the same idea
- vary sentence length and let the narrator sound human, but keep every sentence easy to say aloud
- use commas or short sentences for pauses. Do not use em dashes as stage directions

## Episode Defaults
- overview episodes should build mental model and context
- deep dives should assume the overview already covered the basics
- deep dives should focus on mechanics, tradeoffs, failure modes, and practical engineering understanding
- avoid repeating the same points across overview and deep dive unless repetition is necessary for clarity
